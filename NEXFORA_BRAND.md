## 6. Brand Voice

NEXFORA communicates in a way that is:
- Clear
- Direct
- Confident
- Professional
- Modern
- Helpful
- Business-focused
- Technically capable without unnecessary jargon
- Benefit-led

### Writing Principles

**Be clear.**  
Prefer: “We build booking systems that help businesses manage reservations, availability, customers, and payments.”

Avoid: “We leverage innovative technological ecosystems to revolutionize your booking experience.”

**Be benefit-focused.**  
Do not only describe what NEXFORA builds. Explain why it matters.

**Be confident.**  
Use accurate, direct statements. Avoid weak or uncertain marketing language.

**Be concise.**  
Headlines should communicate one strong idea.

## 7. Logo System

The NEXFORA identity consists of:
1. NEXFORA “N” symbol
2. NEXFORA wordmark
3. DIGITAL INNOVATION descriptor

Approved configurations represented in the brand board:
- Primary stacked logo
- Horizontal logo
- Symbol-only logo
- Wordmark with descriptor
- Light-background version
- Dark-background version
- App icon / favicon treatment

### Light Background
Use the black/dark NEXFORA logo. Indigo may be used for the DIGITAL INNOVATION descriptor where appropriate.

### Dark Background
Use the approved white/light logo treatment with sufficient contrast.

### Symbol Usage
The “N” symbol may be used independently for:
- Favicon
- App icon
- Social profile
- Small digital interfaces
- NEXFORA OS branding
- Loading states
- Branded graphic elements

### Logo Restrictions
Never:
- Stretch or compress the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Change proportions
- Add arbitrary shadows, outlines, or effects
- Apply random colors
- Place it on low-contrast backgrounds
- Modify the symbol geometry
- Replace the wordmark styling
- Crowd the logo with nearby elements

**Note:** Exact clear-space ratios and minimum sizes still require the original vector logo measurements and should be documented once the master SVG assets are finalized.

## 21. AI Brand Rules

All AI agents working on NEXFORA must:

### Always
- Read this document before brand-sensitive work
- Use Inter as the primary typeface
- Use `#0B0D12` as the primary dark color
- Use `#1A1D24` as the secondary dark surface
- Use `#6366F1` as the controlled brand accent
- Use `#E5E7EB` for subtle borders and neutral elements
- Use `#FFFFFF` as the primary light canvas
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Follow a clear grid
- Use short, confident messaging
- Prefer monochromatic visuals with controlled indigo accents
- Use clean outline icons
- Use real product/interface visuals whenever possible
- Maintain a modern, premium, precise appearance

### Never
- Change official brand colors without approval
- Introduce new fonts without approval
- Distort or arbitrarily recolor the logo
- Overuse gradients
- Overuse glassmorphism
- Use excessive rounded cards
- Use generic robot imagery
- Use cyberpunk visual clichés
- Use emoji in professional UI
- Add visual elements without purpose
- Invent clients, testimonials, awards, statistics, case-study results, or unsupported claims
- Invent business information

If critical information is missing: **flag it; do not invent it.**
